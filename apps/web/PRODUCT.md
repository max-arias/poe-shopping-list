# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Path of Exile players assembling a familiar build or upgrading one during a league. They need a quick, trustworthy way to find the official Trade search for common items, then follow inexpensive, practical crafting options when buying is not the best answer.

## Product Purpose

The public catalog turns useful Path of Exile trade searches and affordable crafting knowledge into an open reference. Every recommendation exposes the Trade link and its reasoning so visitors can move from a build need to a purchase or low-cost craft without relying on opaque guide-site claims. Success means a player can locate the right search or craft route quickly and act with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Minimal technical reference: factual, restrained, and practical. The site should feel like a reliable field note written by someone who has done the trade and crafting work, not like a build-guide funnel or a loot showcase. Editorial framing may add orientation, but information and source links stay primary.

## Anti-references

- Build-guide pages that bury the actionable Trade link beneath ads, video embeds, or broad aspirational advice.
- Slick SaaS patterns, marketing-style calls to action, and decorative dashboard metrics.
- Loot-splash spectacle, artificial urgency, and exaggerated rarity visual effects.
- Opaque recommendations that give an item name without the actual search, context, or a cheaper alternative.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead to the action.** Make the official Trade search or craft step easy to locate before surrounding commentary.
2. **Show the reasoning.** A concise applicability note or rationale earns trust and lets players judge whether a recommendation fits their build.
3. **Respect player attention.** Prioritize scanability, direct language, and readable density over promotional storytelling.
4. **Keep the reference open.** Links, exportable data, and practical alternatives should be visible rather than locked behind account, video, or follow prompts.
5. **Use atmosphere as orientation, not obstruction.** The Path of Exile field-manual voice can frame the material, but never competes with searches, prices, or steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline. Preserve strong text contrast, clear keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and headings, and descriptive link labels. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; motion must never be required to reveal instructions or copy a trade query. Keep dense technical content legible for players using zoom, screen readers, or narrow desktop windows.
